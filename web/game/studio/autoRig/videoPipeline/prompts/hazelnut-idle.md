# Hazelnut Level 1 — Idle image-to-video prompt

Use the existing Hazelnut Level 1 image as the sole visual reference. Animate this exact character; do not redesign it.

## Main prompt
Animate this exact Hazelnut character as a polished 2D cartoon game sprite. Preserve its original silhouette, face, shell shape, leaf arrangement, colors, line weight, texture, proportions and identifying details throughout the clip. Keep the character at the same location and scale.

Create a subtle, appealing idle loop: gentle breathing, a very small natural body sway, tiny leaf movement, and a soft blink only if the reference already has eyes. Keep the root/feet planted on one baseline. Motion should be smooth and restrained, returning to a pose compatible with the opening frame so the clip can loop cleanly.

Orthographic 2D view matching the reference. Locked camera, fixed framing, no camera movement, no zoom, no cuts. Show the entire character with margin around it. Center horizontally and align to one ground line. Use a flat pure chroma green background (#00FF00), evenly lit, with no blue spill on the character. No cast shadow touching the background. No other objects. Clean, consistent cartoon game-art rendering.

Duration: 2 seconds. One continuous shot. Include real intermediate character motion, not a still image with camera movement.

## Negative prompt
Do not redesign, reinterpret, morph, rotate, flip, stretch, resize, duplicate, or replace the character. Do not add or remove leaves, facial features, limbs, props, highlights or markings. No character drift, camera shake, zoom, perspective change, scene change, gradient, scenery, floor texture, cast shadow, particles, text, logo, watermark, motion blur, extra characters, or objects crossing in front. Do not crop the character. Do not let its silhouette or colors flicker between frames.

## Review checklist
- Character identity and silhouette remain stable.
- Root stays on one baseline.
- Camera and character scale remain fixed.
- No body part disappears, duplicates, or changes design.
- Opening and ending poses can loop.
- Background can be removed without damaging blue details.
