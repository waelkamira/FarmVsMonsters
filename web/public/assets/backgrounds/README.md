# Farm battlefield background

Place the generated, approved battlefield image in this folder using this exact filename:

`farm-battlefield.jpeg`

## Required image contract
- JPEG, preferably 2560 × 1440 (16:9), opaque background.
- Strict side-view 2D artwork; no isometric or 3D perspective.
- House/protected area on the LEFT; monster entrance on the RIGHT.
- Five horizontal lanes and eight planting columns (40 positions) in the central field.
- Keep all planting positions empty and visually readable.
- No plants, monsters, caveman, princess, UI, text, buttons, health bars, or icons.
- Keep the playable soil area unobstructed.
- The image is background art only. Phaser will draw the interactive grid, characters, plants, monsters, projectiles, HUD, and effects as separate layers.

Do not resize/crop the image after generation without checking that all 5 × 8 positions remain aligned with the gameplay grid. Once the image is uploaded, the Phaser scene will be connected to it and the grid coordinates calibrated against the artwork.
