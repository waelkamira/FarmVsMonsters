# Plant Catalog

- Sunflower: generates energy over time; low durability; no attack.
- Pea Shooter: reliable single-target ranged damage.
- Ice Bloom: slows or briefly freezes enemies in an area.
- Pumpkin Guard: durable defensive blocker.
- Fire Pepper: high-impact area burst with a cooldown.

Each plant needs a 3D model, placement preview, idle/attack/hit/defeat animations as applicable, gameplay prefab, ScriptableObject definition, and tuning values.
