# Game Design Document

## Vision
Farm Vs Monsters is a stylized 3D isometric strategy-defense game combined with a persistent, upgradeable farm. The player prepares a farm, gathers resources, deploys plant and animal defenders, and survives monster waves.

## Core pillars
- Readable tactical combat with meaningful placement and resource decisions.
- A persistent farm that gives purpose to battle rewards.
- Distinct 3D characters, plants, animals, monsters, and bosses.
- Short, replayable stages with clear progression.

## Prototype scope
One farmer; Sunflower, Pea Shooter, Ice Bloom, Pumpkin Guard, Fire Pepper; Sheep and Warrior Goat; five basic monsters plus one boss; one arena; three stages; five waves per stage; two farm buildings; three tools.

## Core loop
Prepare farm → select stage → move and collect energy → place defenders → survive waves → win/lose → receive rewards → return to farm → upgrade → save progress.

## Explicitly deferred
Player marketplace, guilds, chat, PvP, multiplayer, and complex seasonal live operations.
