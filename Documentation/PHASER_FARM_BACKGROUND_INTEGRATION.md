# Phaser Farm Background Integration

## Purpose
Integrate the approved 2D farm artwork as the authoritative battlefield, including its already-painted perspective planting grid.

## Approved background location
`web/public/assets/backgrounds/farm-battlefield.jpeg`

The public URL used by Phaser will be:
`/assets/backgrounds/farm-battlefield.jpeg`

## Art requirements
- 2560 × 1440 JPEG recommended (16:9).
- Strict side-view 2D composition.
- Farmhouse/protected objective at the left.
- Monster gate at the right.
- The uploaded image itself contains a visible perspective planting board with five lanes and eight columns.
- Keep the painted board visible; do not replace it with an axis-aligned overlay grid.
- Keep foreground gameplay silhouettes readable.

## Layer ownership
1. Background image: environment art only.
2. Phaser gameplay layer: grid hit areas, plants, caveman, monsters, projectiles, impacts.
3. HUD layer: energy, wave, health, plant selection, messages.

The background's painted cell seams are visual guides. Phaser maps pointer hit-testing and gameplay coordinates onto the same 5 × 8 board using a bilinear perspective transform.

## Integration checklist after image upload
- [ ] Confirm exact image dimensions and aspect ratio.
- [ ] Inspect the artwork and locate the battlefield bounds.
- [ ] Add the image to the Phaser asset preload pipeline.
- [ ] Scale/crop it to the 1120 × 680 game viewport without distorting characters or cells.
- [ ] Calibrate grid origin, cell width, lane centers, farmhouse target, and monster spawn point to the image.
- [ ] Ensure the caveman, radish, monsters, and projectiles render above the background.
- [ ] Test mouse/touch cell selection against the calibrated grid.
- [ ] Keep a vector fallback if the image fails to load.

## Authoritative perspective-board calibration

The uploaded image was visually inspected at its native 1080 × 576 composition. Its planting grid is part of the artwork, not an empty area awaiting a new rectangular grid. The board is a perspective quadrilateral: the rear edge is narrower and the front edge is wider.

The Phaser background now preserves the image's native 1.875 aspect ratio at 1120 × 597.33 and is vertically centered in the 1120 × 680 viewport. The playable board is mapped from four calibrated screen-space corners:

- Rear-left: (388, 360)
- Rear-right: (824, 362)
- Front-right: (887, 578)
- Front-left: (259, 579)

The 8 × 5 cells are generated by bilinear interpolation between those corners. Pointer selection uses the inverse bilinear transform, and plant placement, lane centers, monster movement, blocker detection, and caveman lane lookup use the same mapping. This replaces the previous incorrect X-axis orthographic tilt and the old axis-aligned 600 × 230 board.

These corner coordinates are a first calibration against the visible painted seams. They still require in-browser visual validation and may need small adjustments to sit exactly on every seam. Do not reintroduce a second, straight rectangular grid over the artwork.
