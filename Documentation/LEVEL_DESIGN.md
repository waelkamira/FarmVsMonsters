# Level Design

## Initial scenes
- Boot: initialize settings and save data.
- MainMenu: continue/new game and stage access.
- Farm: persistent hub, buildings, resources, and upgrades.
- Battle: arena, deployment, waves, combat, and results.
- WorldMap: stage selection and progression.
- Test: isolated mechanics validation.

## First content slice
One arena reused across three difficulty-tuned stages. Each stage contains five authored waves and one final boss encounter in the prototype progression. Keep navigation and objectives legible on both desktop and touch devices.
