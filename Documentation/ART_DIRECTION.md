# Art Direction

## Visual target
High-quality stylized 3D with soft forms, appealing proportions, saturated but controlled colors, expressive silhouettes, and readable combat effects. Use an isometric 3D camera with real volumetric models—not flat sprites placed in a 3D scene.

## Asset requirements
Characters, plants, animals, monsters, buildings, and props need full 3D meshes, materials, lighting, shadows, and suitable rigs/animations. Models must read correctly from multiple camera angles.

## Production priorities
1. Establish a consistent scale and palette.
2. Build one polished farm environment and one battle arena.
3. Create a modular character/enemy rig and animation set.
4. Make gameplay-critical silhouettes and effects readable before decorative detail.
5. Optimize materials, meshes, and effects for mobile.
