# Monster Catalog

## Implemented ordinary monster

- **Monster 01** — the sole currently approved ordinary enemy.
- Artwork: `web/public/assets/characters/monsters/01/01.png`.
- The same source image is mirrored into Godot at `godot/assets/characters/monsters/01/01.png`.
- It uses the shared ordinary-enemy movement, plant-blocking, bite, damage, freeze, and defeat rules.
- The dragon remains a separate boss encounter and is not part of the ordinary monster roster.

Future monsters are not considered implemented, playable, spawnable, or available in the Studio base-archetype selector until their own approved artwork, behavior, combat profile, and tests are delivered.
